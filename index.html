      Playlists

      Build setlists outside Planning Center from any song in the church's Planning Center library, so chord charts still work.           Sound device

          The phone, tablet or computer that plays the pad and click, the one plugged into the board. Only one device makes sound. The list shows devices that have been signed in during the last 15 minutes.

          Who can control

          Pick what each person can control from their own device. Songs moves everyone to a song, Pad and Click start, stop and change them on the sound device, and Chart lets them tap a section or page to show it on everyone's screen. The leader controls everything, from any device they're signed in on.

          Following devices show the leader's song and chart and never make sound. Anyone can look ahead at another song and tap "Back to" the leader to rejoin.

          Layout on this device

          Choose what to show, so the sound booth, drummer, keys player and guitar or bass can each see just what they need.

          Quick picks

          Or pick and choose

          With the chord chart on, the setlist shows as a row of song buttons above the chart. Drag the bar between the controls and the chart to resize them.

          Save this layout

          Saves what's showing, the divider position, and the chart text size and columns. Pick a saved layout above to edit or delete it.

          Team

          Everyone signs in with their own PIN. Viewers see chord charts. Editors can also fix songs and pick the Planning Center service. Admins also manage this list. Every change is recorded with the person's name.
